🛡️ AI Ethics & Responsible AI

Essential ethical considerations for deploying natural language processing models

⚖️ 1. Bias

Machine learning models inherit societal and textual biases present within training corpora. Regular audits are mandatory.

📊 2. Data Quality

Poor, noisy, or unbalanced training data can produce skewed confidence scores and unreliable real-world predictions.

🔒 3. Privacy

User input text should be processed securely without storing sensitive personal identifiable information (PII).

👁️ 4. Transparency

Users must be informed clearly that predictions are produced by probabilistic algorithms, not authoritative human decisions.

🔍 5. Explainability

Exposing feature importance weights empowers users to understand why an AI model arrived at a specific conclusion.

🤝 6. Human Oversight

AI predictions must serve as decision-support assistance, leaving final accountability with human decision-makers.

⚠️ 7. Misuse

Text classifiers should not be treated as infallible systems or misused for automated censorship without review.

⚙️ How It Works – System Architecture

Click on any step below to explore the end-to-end NLP & ML processing pipeline

1. User Text Input ▼
The user inputs a natural language sentence (e.g. "I absolutely love this application!").
2. NLP Preprocessing ▼
The raw string is converted to lowercase, special characters and punctuation are removed.
3. Tokenization & Stemming ▼
Text is split into discrete word tokens. Common stop-words are filtered out, and Porter Stemmer normalizes words to root forms.
4. TF-IDF Feature Extraction ▼
Tokens are mapped into numerical sparse vectors based on Term Frequency (TF) and Inverse Document Frequency (IDF) relative to the training set.
5. Machine Learning Classifier ▼
The TF-IDF vector is fed into the selected classifier (Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes, or Linear SVM) to calculate decision scores.
6. Prediction & Confidence ▼
The highest probability class (Positive, Negative, or Neutral) is selected as the prediction output alongside the confidence score.
7. Explainable AI (XAI) Attribution ▼
Model weight parameters are extracted to identify positive and negative contributing words, highlighting their influence on the prediction.
